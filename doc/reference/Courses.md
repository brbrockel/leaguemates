# Courses

Courses within about 15 miles of Berlin, CT, researched 2026-10-15 and loaded into the app with `api/scripts/import-course-cards.ts`. Each nine has par, men's stroke index and White-tee yards (or the nearest tee when there are no White tees; the course's nine shows which). Card pictures, where the club publishes one, are on the course page.

**Confidence:** *official card* is the club's own scorecard; *multiple sources agree* and *single source* come from GolfPass, Stymie, OpenGolfAPI and similar. Check single-source courses against a printed card before the league plays them. Timberlin is in [Timberlin.md](Timberlin.md).

**Rolling Greens** (checked against its card, 2026-10-16): the card numbers its men's handicaps across 18 holes (two loops of the nine), so the nine's stroke indexes are 7, 2, 16, 10, 6, 13, 14, 18, 15 rather than 1–9. That's correct: strokes go to holes in that order, hardest (hole 2) first. OpenGolfAPI's 5, 3, 15, 13, 7, 11, 1, 17, 9 matches neither this card nor the club's older one.
