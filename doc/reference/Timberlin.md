# Timberlin Golf Club scorecard

Timberlin Golf Club, Berlin, CT 06037. 18-hole municipal course, designed by Al Zikorus (1970), par 72. The league plays one nine per week, alternating between the front and back nines.

Collected 2026-10-15. Par, stroke index and White yardages were checked against the club's official scorecard ([timberlingolf.com/course/scorecard](https://www.timberlingolf.com/course/scorecard)). Pictures of that card are on the course page in the app (loaded with `add-course-image`; they aren't kept in this repository because the card carries advertisers).

- [Official scorecard](https://www.timberlingolf.com/course/scorecard): every tee, par, handicap, ratings
- [OpenGolfAPI: Timberlin Golf Club](https://courses.opengolfapi.org/courses/ct/timberlin-golf-club): par, Black-tee yardage, stroke index
- [NCCGA tournament tee sheet (Blue tees)](https://nccga.org/app/golf-tournaments/7123/tee_times/round/2): par, Blue yardage, stroke index, a second yardage row labelled W
- [Stymie](https://stymie.golf/golf-courses/connecticut/hartford-county/berlin/timberlin-golf-course) and [18Birdies](https://18birdies.com/golf-courses/club/92962d90-86ac-11e4-8c28-020000005b00/timberlin-golf-course): course details. 18Birdies gives different Blue yardages.
